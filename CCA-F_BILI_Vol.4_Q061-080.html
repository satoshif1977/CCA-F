Redefine the fields as strings with an enum constraint — e.g., ["PRESENT", "ABSENT", "UNKNOWN"] — to explicitly represent all three states.

✓ 正解

🇺🇸 English
A legal research agent is instructed to output a confidence score from 0–100 and call escalate_to_senior when the score is below 80. Why is this approach fundamentally unreliable?
✓ Correct Answer
Self-reported confidence scores are not a reliable proxy for actual case complexity, as LLMs frequently hallucinate their own confidence levels.
🔑 Key Words

calibration※ 関連概念キャリブレーション確信度スコアが統計的な正解率に対応している状態。LLMはキャリブレーションされていない

🇯🇵 Japanese
